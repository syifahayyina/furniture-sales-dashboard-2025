# 2025 Furniture Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive furniture sales dashboard built with Looker Studio, using Excel for data preparation and exploratory analysis. It analyzes 10,000 orders from January to December 2025 to uncover insights into revenue trends, product and room category performance, regional contribution, and order completion. The goal is to help business stakeholders understand what drives revenue and where opportunities lie.

## Project Objectives
1. Track monthly revenue trends and identify the peak and lowest months
2. Summarize overall performance : total revenue, total orders, completion rate, and average order value
3. Identify the categories and products that contribute the most revenue
4. Compare units sold against revenue share to find high-volume but low-revenue products, and vice versa
5. Evaluate revenue and completed orders across cities
